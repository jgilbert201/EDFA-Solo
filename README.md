# EDFA Solo

EDFA Solo (https://edfa.uk) is a companion app for Elite Dangerous. It reads your game journals and turns them into a personal record of where you have been, what you have found, and what you are working on. It also gives you planning tools for exploration, combat, trading, ships, carriers and colonisation. The main app runs on your own PC. You can use its local features without an EDFA account, then connect an account if you want syncing and shared features.

Sign up and **download the client here**  - https://edfa.uk

## Exploration Operations

Exploration Operations helps you survey the system you are in. It brings your scans, mapping, biology, samples and past visits into one view, so you can see what is known and what is still worth checking.

**Features**

- See scanned stars, planets, moons, rings and biological signals, with clear progress for each body.
- Find bodies worth mapping and see possible biological species. Predictions are marked as predictions until your scans identify them.
- Track biological samples and the distance needed before taking the next one.
- Use a priority view to focus on unfinished work, valuable mapping and useful materials.
- Revisit a system and see the survey work you recorded there before.
- Browse exploration history and estimates of biological data you have yet to sell.
- Use the in-game HUD for route, survey and surface guidance.

## Wayfinder Operations

Wayfinder helps you find interesting places to visit and turn them into a trip. It is for commanders who want a reason to head somewhere, as well as a route to follow.

**Features**

- Browse an attributed catalogue of places of interest by starting system, distance and interests.
- See all matching places, choose suggested stops, and save the ones you want to visit.
- Build an ordered trip and review route changes before accepting them.
- Keep destination notes and guides available after they have been downloaded.
- Track system arrivals separately from visits to the actual site.
- See the current destination in Exploration and use the existing trip and clipboard controls.
- Record research observations and follow guidance for an optional external Canonn submission.

## Combat Operations

Combat Operations keeps your recorded ship combat missions and rewards together. It uses journal evidence where the game provides it and lets you correct a mission count against what you see in game.

**Features**

- Browse massacre and assassination missions, including targets, deadlines, rewards and recorded status.
- Filter active, ready to hand in and historical missions.
- See estimated massacre progress from matching recorded kills, or enter the current count shown in game.
- Identify a recorded assassination target when its pilot and faction match the mission.
- Review recorded bounty and combat bond awards, cash-ins and estimates of vouchers still held.
- Choose which missions appear in the mission HUD.
- Use a live target HUD and a compact mission HUD while flying in normal space.

## Trading Operations

Trading Operations helps you plan a trade and follow it through. It uses your recorded ship, cargo, location and funds alongside dated market information.

**Features**

- Plan a single trade, return trip, supply run or longer trading journey.
- Set a budget and reserve, then check suggested loads against cargo space, stock, demand and station access.
- See where a market quote came from and how old it is.
- Save a run and follow purchases, sales, deliveries and losses from your journals.
- Keep spending, sale proceeds and estimated profit separate.
- See the next stop and run progress in the native HUD.
- Copy the next route system automatically if you enable that option.

## Carrier Operations

Carrier Operations is a private workspace for your fleet carrier. It combines its recorded status with expedition history, route planning and Tritium estimates.

**Features**

- See your owned carrier’s recorded location, status and jump history.
- Plan and save carrier routes, then follow jumps confirmed by your journals.
- Estimate Tritium needs using tank fuel, cargo fuel and your chosen reserve.
- Review carrier finances and expedition totals, including recorded biological sales and unsold estimates.
- Keep uncertain or missing records visible instead of treating them as completed jumps or confirmed income.
- Share your carrier’s location with eligible people through EDFA if you choose to.

## Fleet

Fleet gives you a record of the ships you own and the loadouts Solo has observed. It is a place to check what you have before starting another build.

**Features**

- Browse owned ships and their recorded modules.
- See suggested roles for ship models and set your own role for a particular ship.
- Use ship context to choose suitable HUD defaults.
- Export known ship builds in SLEF format or open them in EDSY or Coriolis.

## Outfitting Operations

Outfitting Operations helps you work from a target build to a practical shopping and engineering plan.

**Features**

- Import a build and plan for a new ship or one you already own.
- Keep up to 20 named build projects.
- Check funding, required modules, materials and engineering work.
- See known material stock, proposed trades and direct collection sources.
- Find reported module stock with its observation date.
- Plan shopping and engineering stops from a chosen starting system.
- Track engineer, Guardian module and Powerplay unlock information, with manual corrections where the journal record is incomplete.

## Colonisation Operations

Colonisation Operations is a workspace for planning systems, construction sites and the supplies needed to build them. It follows recorded construction progress while leaving room for the details you need to enter yourself.

**Features**

- Plan projects across ordered systems, with sites, facilities and build steps.
- See construction progress, supply needs, build order and projected points.
- Work with system and body maps, site details and estimated facility effects.
- Save and compare planning scenarios.
- Use recorded depot and delivery evidence to update known requirements.
- Search markets for needed commodities and plan grouped loads for several sites.
- Compare remaining demand with ship cargo, entered carrier stock and crew reservations.
- See the current construction site or selected load in the optional colony HUD.
- Link an EDFA account to sync your projects and work with permitted crew members.

## Material Supplies

Material Supplies shows what your commander has collected and helps you spot places to replenish it.

**Features**

- See recorded raw, manufactured and encoded material stock.
- Set a low-stock threshold and preferences for gravity and raw-material abundance.
- Let Exploration highlight suitable landable bodies with materials you are running low on.
- Choose how much material shopping appears in Exploration, from off to full.

## Ship Journeys

Ship Journeys is a planner for long-distance travel in your own ship. Its stops are strategic waypoints that you review and plot in Elite Dangerous.

**Features**

- Plan ordinary or neutron-assisted journeys using Spansh.
- Enter your ship’s range, scoop and other planning assumptions.
- Review a calculated route before saving it.
- Follow recorded FSD jumps, while keeping manual skips separate from arrivals.
- Pause and resume a journey without counting travel that happened while it was paused.
- Keep saved routes readable offline and copy the next waypoint when needed.

## Codex, Discoveries and Records

These views turn your recorded discoveries into a personal collection you can browse later.

**Features**

- Browse your Codex entries and discovery history.
- Find unfinished surveys by commander, region, evidence or system.
- See historical sampling work without assuming an old partial sample is still active in game.
- Review commander honours and Galactic Records drawn from eligible recorded activity.
- Use the EDSM Codex recovery helper for personal confirmations when original journal coverage is missing.

## CMDR Scrapbook

CMDR Scrapbook lets you save a moment from Elite Dangerous when you choose to capture it.

**Features**

- Take a screenshot with an assigned shortcut or Button Box control while Elite is in focus.
- Keep the original image and a thumbnail on your Solo PC.
- Add a title, notes and an optional discovery association.
- Search and browse your moments in a timeline.
- Link EDFA to back up moments to your account and choose whether an individual moment appears on your shared commander profile.

## Button Box

Button Box turns a tablet, phone or browser window into a custom set of controls for Elite Dangerous. Solo runs on the gaming PC and sends the button presses there.

**Features**

- Start with example layouts for flight, fighter, SRV and on-foot play, or copy the Exploration, Combat and Mining templates.
- Create layouts with your own labels, icons, images and button sizes.
- Assign an Elite keyboard binding or a custom key to a button.
- Choose layouts manually or enable switching when Solo detects a change between ship, fighter, SRV and on-foot play.
- Edit layouts on desktop and use a simpler controller view on a tablet or phone.
- Back up and import layouts, including their artwork.
- Open the controller over your home network after enabling home network access via any device which runs a web browser.

## In-game HUD and shortcuts

Solo can show information over Elite Dangerous while you play. You choose which panels to use and where they sit.

**Features**

- Show route and arrival information, survey targets, sample separation, surface guidance, colony supplies, trading progress and combat information where relevant.
- Move and resize panels in preview mode, then return them to click-through play mode.
- Set panel visibility, scale, appearance and guidance preferences.
- Use shortcuts for actions such as changing survey targets, saving a surface waypoint and capturing a Scrapbook moment.
- Set up three quick-switch slots for Operations, with manual choices or your three most recently used workspaces.
- Assign keyboard or supported controller buttons to quick-switch actions.

## Config & Link

Config & Link is where you set up Solo for your game installation and choose which online services to use.

**Features**

- Select your Elite Dangerous journal folder and watch journal processing progress.
- Manage the local app, HUD, shortcuts, home network access and update preferences.
- Find and review Elite control bindings, make local backups, and apply a revised preset while Elite is closed.
- Link an EDFA account to sync eligible history and workspaces or download retained account history.
- Connect Frontier for supported account data without requiring an EDFA account.
- Set up optional EDSM, Inara, EDDN, EDAstro and Canonn sharing, each with its own choices.
- Open local logs when you need to investigate a problem.

## EDFA account and shared features

Solo works locally first. Linking it to EDFA adds account-backed history, recovery and social features for commanders who want them.

**Features**

- Sync eligible journal history and selected Solo workspaces across linked installations.
- Keep Colonisation projects, Material Supplies and Ship Journeys available through your account.
- Back up Game controls and Scrapbook moments to private account storage.
- Use Wingmates, crews and permitted comparisons.
- Share your recorded commander or carrier position with eligible people when location sharing is enabled.
- See your commander honours and shared profile information.
